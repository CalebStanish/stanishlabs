Website services

Everything your business needs to get online—and stay there.

Custom sites, content and domain setup, and ongoing hosting and care—handled by one person, start to finish. No page-builder templates, no agency handoffs.

01

Custom business websites

A fast, uncluttered multi-page website designed around your business, your customers, and the work you want more of. Every site is hand-built for the business it belongs to—not assembled from a generic template.

Includes a home page, service and about pages, a working contact form, mobile-responsive design, and the on-page basics that help search engines find you: fast load times, clean structure, and proper page titles and descriptions.

02

Content and domain setup

Bring your finished copy or start with rough notes—either works. I help organize the pages, shape the wording so it reads clearly, and connect your domain and DNS so the site is reachable at your own address.

If you don't have a domain yet, I can help you register one. If you already have one elsewhere, I'll help point it here without downtime.

Monthly hosting, uptime monitoring, backups, security updates, and routine text and photo updates—handled by the same person who built your site. If something breaks or needs to change, you email one person and it gets done.
